Our Business

Mineral Import and Export Trade

Specializing in the cross-border import and export of non-ferrous metals and mineral raw materials. Leveraging the geographical advantage of Lianyungang Port, we stably connect global mineral resources and overseas buyers, providing compliant customs clearance, bulk procurement and long-term supply cooperation.

矿产品进出口贸易

专注各类有色金属、矿石原材料跨境进出口业务，依托连云港港口区位优势，稳定对接全球矿山货源与海外采购商，提供合规报关、批量集采、长期供货协议服务。

Pharmaceutical Intermediate Supplier

Focusing on fine chemicals and pharmaceutical intermediates supply chain. We cooperate with qualified manufacturers with strict quality control on purity and specifications, providing stable stock, customized production and compliant export services for global pharmaceutical and chemical enterprises.

医药中间体供应商

深耕精细化工与医药中间体供应链，对接优质生产工厂，严格把控产品纯度与品质标准，可为海外药企、化工企业提供稳定现货、定制代工与合规出口配套服务。

International Logistics Services

We provide one-stop cross-border logistics solutions including sea freight, air freight, booking, customs declaration and inspection, tailored for bulk cargo such as minerals, chemicals and industrial products.

国际物流服务

提供一站式跨境物流方案，涵盖海运、空运、订舱、报关报检、跨境货运代理等服务，适配矿产品、化工品、工业品大宗货物跨境运输需求。

Warehousing & Transportation

Supported by local port warehousing resources, we provide integrated services including cargo storage, sorting, loading and unloading, inland transportation and port consolidation, ensuring efficient turnover and stable delivery.

仓储运输服务

依托港口本地仓储资源，提供货物仓储、分拣、装卸、内陆运输、港口集运一体化服务，保障货物周转高效、交付稳定。

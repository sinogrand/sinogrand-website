About Us | SINOGRAND

SINOGRAND INTERNATIONAL SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT CO., LTD., located in Lianyungang, a major port city of China, is a comprehensive supply chain enterprise integrating mineral import & export, pharmaceutical intermediate supply, international freight forwarding and warehousing transportation.

Adhering to the principle of integrity and long-term win-win cooperation, SINOGRAND connects global mineral resources and industrial raw material demand markets. Relying on Lianyungang port’s unique geographical advantage, we provide global partners with stable supply, strict quality control, flexible shipment and one-stop cross-border supply chain solutions.

Our team has rich experience in international trade and cross-border logistics. We are committed to transparent communication, reliable delivery and customized services to support the sustained growth of our clients.

连云港中泰国际供应链管理有限公司坐落于中国重要港口城市连云港，是一家集矿产品进出口、医药中间体供应、国际货运代理、仓储运输为一体的综合性供应链企业。

中泰国际秉持诚信经营、长期共赢的合作理念，搭建全球矿产资源与工业原材料需求市场的贸易桥梁。依托连云港港口独特区位优势，为全球合作伙伴提供稳定货源、严格品控、灵活出货以及一站式跨境供应链解决方案。

公司团队拥有丰富的国际贸易与跨境物流实操经验，坚持透明沟通、稳定交付、定制化服务，助力客户业务持续增长。
